Actuators worn on or near the body should be light, compliant, and safe. I work on soft and smart-material actuators that meet these needs.

### Shape memory alloy (SMA) artificial muscles

SMA spring actuators are light and compliant, which makes them attractive as artificial muscles. I used bundles of SMA springs as artificial muscles that drive an anatomically inspired prosthetic wrist {% cite hyeon2023wrist chung2023roman %} and wearable robots for the shoulder and elbow {% cite hyeon2022shoulder chung2023ur chung2024origami jeong2022elbow %}. The springs provide both active force when heated and passive restoring force without energy input.

### Electrohydraulic actuators (EPIC)

Electrohydraulic actuators powered by induced interfacial charges (EPIC) generate large forces with soft materials. I designed a self-sensing EPIC actuator that measures its own state through a capacitance sensing circuit {% cite chung2022ur %}, and contributed to improving actuator durability with a skin-inspired hybrid polymer film {% cite kim2022mrs %}.

### Stretchable and transparent thermal devices

A soft dielectric heater made of PVC-gel with hydrogel electrodes stays transparent and keeps a uniform temperature even when stretched far beyond its original length. It can be scaled to a row/column addressable 5 x 5 heater array that delivers different thermal patterns {% cite kim2024pvcgel %}.
