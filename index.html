I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Ki-Uk Kyung in the Human-Robot Interaction Lab.

My research focuses on tactile sensors shared by humans and robots. I design optical fiber-based fingertip sensors that can be worn on a human hand during demonstration and mounted on a robot hand during execution. The same sensor captures contact location, force magnitude, and force distribution in both settings, so that tactile information from human demonstrations can be transferred directly to robotic manipulation. Because the signal travels as light, these sensors are immune to electromagnetic interference.

I have also developed optical fiber-based force myography (FMG) systems for hand gesture recognition, joint angle estimation, and grasping force estimation, and soft wearable and rehabilitation robots driven by shape memory alloy (SMA) artificial muscles.
