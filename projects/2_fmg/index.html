Optical Fiber Force Myography

Thin, flexible FMG sensors for gesture recognition and grasping force estimation

Force myography (FMG) estimates hand motion and grip from the small pressure changes that forearm muscles produce on the skin. I developed thin and flexible FMG sensors made of weaved optical fiber, in which light loss through controlled fiber-polymer contact encodes the applied force.

Starting from a proof-of-concept sensor (Chung et al., 2024) and a sleeve-type system (Chung et al., 2024), the work grew into a versatile wearable FMG system for human-robot interaction (Chung et al., 2025), with applications to hand gesture recognition, finger joint angle estimation, and grasping force estimation (Chung et al., 2025; Chung et al., 2025). Part of this work was carried out during my visit to the MERIIT Lab at New York University.
