Soft Actuators

SMA artificial muscles, electrohydraulic actuators, and stretchable thermal devices

Actuators worn on or near the body should be light, compliant, and safe. I work on soft and smart-material actuators that meet these needs.

Shape memory alloy (SMA) artificial muscles

SMA spring actuators are light and compliant, which makes them attractive as artificial muscles. I used bundles of SMA springs as artificial muscles that drive an anatomically inspired prosthetic wrist (Hyeon et al., 2023; Chung et al., 2023) and wearable robots for the shoulder and elbow (Hyeon et al., 2022; Chung et al., 2023; Chung et al., 2024; Jeong et al., 2022). The springs provide both active force when heated and passive restoring force without energy input.

Electrohydraulic actuators (EPIC)

Electrohydraulic actuators powered by induced interfacial charges (EPIC) generate large forces with soft materials. I designed a self-sensing EPIC actuator that measures its own state through a capacitance sensing circuit (Chung et al., 2022), and contributed to improving actuator durability with a skin-inspired hybrid polymer film (Kim et al., 2022).

Stretchable and transparent thermal devices

A soft dielectric heater made of PVC-gel with hydrogel electrodes stays transparent and keeps a uniform temperature even when stretched far beyond its original length. It can be scaled to a row/column addressable 5 x 5 heater array that delivers different thermal patterns (Kim et al., 2024).
