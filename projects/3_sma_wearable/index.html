SMA-Driven Wearable Robots

Lightweight wearable and rehabilitation robots with shape memory alloy artificial muscles

Shape memory alloy (SMA) spring actuators are light and compliant, which makes them attractive as artificial muscles for wearable robots. During my M.S. study, I worked on SMA-driven devices for upper limb assistance and rehabilitation.

  • Shoulder abduction assistance. A four-bar linkage-based support mechanism (Hyeon et al., 2022), followed by a double-petal mechanism based on petal fold origami that follows the scapulohumeral rhythm while providing high mechanical gain (Chung et al., 2023; Chung et al., 2024).
  • Prosthetic wrist. A lightweight, flexible two-DOF prosthetic wrist that combines an elliptic rolling joint with SMA artificial muscles (Hyeon et al., 2023; Chung et al., 2023).
  • Elbow and forearm assistance. A soft wearable robot assisting elbow flexion and forearm supination/pronation (Jeong et al., 2022).
