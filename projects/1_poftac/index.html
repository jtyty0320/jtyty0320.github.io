Shared Human-Robot Tactile Sensing

Optical fiber fingertip tactile sensors for human-to-robot manipulation skill transfer

Transferring human manipulation skills to robot hands needs more than hand kinematics. The robot also needs to know where contact happens, how strong it is, and how the force is distributed over the fingertip.

PofTac is a plastic optical fiber-based tactile sensor designed to be used on both sides of this transfer. The same sensor is worn on a human fingertip while a person demonstrates a task and is mounted on a robot fingertip when the robot executes it, so the tactile data from both are directly comparable (Chung et al., 2026; Chung & Kyung, 2026). Optical signal transmission makes the sensor immune to electromagnetic interference.

This line of work is the topic of my Ph.D. thesis, Design of Optical Fiber-based Fingertip Tactile Sensor for Human-Robot Manipulation Skill Transfer. Ongoing work extends the sensing to robotic fingernails for improved manipulation with robot hands.
